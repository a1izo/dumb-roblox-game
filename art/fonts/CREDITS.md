# Font credits

Inkbound's lettering (`art/scripts/ui/fonts.py`) is drawn from two fonts that ship with Roblox
Studio. Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org). The inked faces made from them use their own names
(Inkbound Gothic, Specter Scrawl), as the license asks for modified versions.

| Face | Drawn from | Copyright |
|---|---|---|
| Inkbound Gothic | Grenze Gotisch Bold | Copyright 2020 The Grenze Gotisch Project Authors (https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Grenze-Gotisch) |
| Specter Scrawl | Amatic SC Bold | Copyright 2015 The Amatic SC Project Authors (https://github.com/googlefonts/AmaticSC) |

The fonts themselves are not in this repository; the script reads them from the local Roblox
Studio install.
