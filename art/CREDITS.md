# Source material

The photographed material the game's surfaces, skies and printed matter are made from. The files
themselves are not in the repository (`art/source/`, fetched by `art/scripts/sources.py`); every
one is listed with its address, size and checksum in `art/sources.json`.

Nothing here was made by an image generator. Thank you to the people who photographed, scanned and
gave these away.
