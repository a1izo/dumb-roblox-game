were fine; Sam's pale slab at the blade lock (not traced: a Trail sheet or its edge Trail, `Sam.luau` ~265-300) and the checkmateCoven down shot were left.

**Test without a script sync (new).** The place's Client holds its own module copies, so `require` from `execute_luau` (Client) gives a SEPARATE copy: flipping `Pilot.on` there does not reach the game's cut. Two ways round it:
1. A camera: bind a render step above the camera director, `RunService:BindToRenderStep("ovr", Enum.RenderPriority.Last.Value + 50, ...)`, with `CFrame.lookAt(O + eye, O + look)`; the win/scene stage origin is `Workspace.DeathsGambitStage.Set.Floor.Position + (0, 0.25, 0)`, which for every cut seen was (6000, 600, -6000), identity rotation (Floor-frame numbers from `WinCuts.luau` add straight on). Bind it BEFORE the click and unbind after 25 s with a `task.delay` (a script that waits for the new Floor can miss: the previous cut's Floor is still there). Unbind (`pcall`) before binding again.
2. An effect: `require(PlayerScripts.Client.Presentation.WinCinema)` in the same call, `rawset` the flags on that copy's Pilot, then call `W.ACTS.fx(refs, event)` with `refs = {origin = CFrame, parts = {}, props = {}, cleanups = {}, rows = {}}` at points in front of the camera on a loop (kinds side by side at different x), old build with the flags off for comparison. Requiring `WinCinema` this way made the Debug panel vanish from that play (start a new play to get it back); it also works in the lobby, whose grey light shows the shapes.

**Timing traps.** Between two tool calls the wall clock runs 3 to 8 s (your own thinking): an 8 s cut is over by the second capture. Do the click and `wait` in one call and the first capture next; a cut shows about 2.5 s of black / fade-in at its start. Hold an override for the whole cut rather than aiming at a second. The gallery buttons used: GUI y = picture y minus 58, scene deaths 202 (chip 765, clock 889, dark 986), 227 (blackwall 741, walrider 827, sans 908), 252 (sam 741, god 831, ironMan 946), 277 (requiem 751, patriot 829, selection 907); wins 314 (finalPage 745, newWorld 840, caseClosed 924), 339 (checkmate 741), 376 (caseClosedCoven 752, checkmateCoven 854, finalPageBureau 929), 401 (newWorldBureau 757).
