Asked 2026-11-07: all 14 deaths redone ("very high quality"), the 5 death styles given actings of their own, and a
shop Preview scene for every custom death and death style.

- **art/scripts/deathkit.py**: `rod_fall` (a body tipping about its knees, hips or toes under gravity, g = 35
  studs/s²), `body_key`/`floor_key`/`kneel_key`/`sit_key`/`prone_key`/`supine_key` place the body by its HIPS (a knee or
  hip stays put while the body tips over it; placing by the root offset slid the knees a stud as the torso turned),
  `Feet` + `stand` (real steps: each rigid leg leans to its sole, never slides sideways out of the hip), `lead_arm`
  (the arm on the falling side must lead or the hand props the body up), `shiver`, `breaths`.
- **Trap: root=(pitch, yaw, roll) leans in the MARK's frame** (CFrame.Angles is Rx·Ry·Rz): a body turned 150° and
  "leaning forward" tips over backwards. `deathkit.turned(pitch, yaw, roll)` gives the angles for yaw-then-lean.
- **deaths.py** (the 14 + diveCrash, which keeps the old accident for the rift dive) and **fallen.py**
  (fallen<Ash|Strings|Crows|Stone|Glass><Down|Up>: Down from a body face down, Up on its back) write
  `src/shared/Anim/DeathTiming.luau` (hit, fx, still, length; the style clips' moments). After changing a death, run
  `run_anims.py -- --add <clips>` and then `blender -b --python art/scripts/cinema/deathcuts.py` (the shop scenes are
  aimed from the clips).
- **Presentation/Fallen**: a styled death in a case is played on every screen by a copy (Stage.copyOf) that collapses,
  lies still `ManualTiming.STYLE_AFTER` and acts the style; `Fallen.act` re-bases the style clip on the torso where it
  lies (moves the anchored root part) and eases the limbs over 0.45 s. The server marks the body `LDeathStyle`, so its
  frozen copy (`Body_<alias>`) is hidden where the copy shows, and holds the case for the acting (DeathNoteService).
- **Shop scenes**: `art/scripts/cinema/deathcuts.py` writes `src/shared/Cinema/DeathCuts.luau` (WinCuts format plus
  clipAt and the events book/lifeless/deathFx/fallen/card), played by WinCinema (`WinCinema.cutOf(item)`); a framing
  check prints any shot that loses the head or body under the letterbox; `art/scripts/review_deaths.py` renders sheets.
- **Trap: the star faces -z and every camera stands in front of it, so the background is +z.** Dressing placed at -z
  sits behind the cameras and never shows (the first pass did this for every set); `dress()` mirrors it behind the star
  and builds the room round it with 13 studs kept clear in front for the cameras. Keep |x| < 2.5 from -6 to +5 in z
  clear of dressing: that is where the bodies fall (forward up to 5 studs, back up to 3.5, the dark drags 4.5 back).
- Checked by the harness probe Deaths (scratchpad harness: every scene to its end, the torso's lowest point over the
  floor, the stand-in for each style).
