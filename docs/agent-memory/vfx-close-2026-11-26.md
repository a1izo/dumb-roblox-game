**What was done.** `docs/vfx.md` now describes the kit, the library (Fx v6), the booth and report, the Pilot flag table
(every flag, with a reason on each OFF one), the house rules, how to test and what is open, plus a Removal plan. The plan's
Status block says Stages 0 to 4 are done as far as booth and lobby pictures go (4f partial) and Stage 5 is closed safely.

**Why nothing was deleted (V5.1 said to delete unused old textures and `FxKit`).** By grep on 2026-11-26 every old texture
still has a live caller; `Presentation/FxKit` has 4 requirers (`EmoteFx/Godhood`, `Props`, `Strolls`: only in the old branches
of ON flags; `WinCinema`: two unflagged spots); `Vfx/Recipes` has 38 call sites, 15 of them in the nine
`Presentation/SceneDeaths/*` files, which read no flag. Every OFF flag also falls back to the old code, so deleting it would
change what plays. The only old texture with one referrer is `VfxCandleFlame` (`Recipes.luau:455`, the candle kind).

**Counts.** 101 flags in `Vfx/Pilot.luau`: 54 ON, 47 OFF. `check_textures`: 33 pass, 16 fail, all 16 old sheets (the plan's
log and some briefs said 15; the script says 16: VfxAsh, CandleFlame, Chunk, Circle, Column, Crack, Dust, Feather, Flame,
Moth, Page, Ring, RingBand, Slash, Smoke, Wisp).

**Why it matters / how to apply.** A future "delete the old VFX" job starts from the Removal plan in `docs/vfx.md`: move the
scene deaths and `WinCinema`'s unflagged spots to `Fresh` first, delete the old branches of the flags the owner has seen ON,
then `FxKit`, then `Recipes`, then the sheets nothing names (one Fx/Core bump, update `art/budget.json`). Deleting is the
owner's call, as is the fire (redraw and re-import, the cheap Luau route, or keep the old flame). Related:
[[vfx-rework-plan-2026-11]], [[fx-v6-fire-redo]], [[vfx-rollout-4f]], [[vfx-rollout-4e]].
