# Death's hand: what comes next

The shop as it stands (2026-10-23): a table of tarot cards in suits, Today's hand dealt face down with
the day's deal at a quarter off, matching sets with titles (emote sets too), collection milestones, and
styles that play in the case itself (writing, verdict, death), Deathsinger forms, hats, Manual covers
(each card shows the cover's own design), win cutscenes (one for each side) and seats and chairs for the
war table. Browsing: search, show and order, "New" ribbons, posed emote cards, a wishlist, Fate's weekly
draw and the Season ledger's free line; a mannequin by the lobby spawn shows the day's emote. Coins only:
the Robux suit stays hidden until a product id is set in `src/shared/Products.luau`.

Its look is the owner's direction A, "Death's Hand" (picked 2026-10-21): a hand of three card backs by the
coins; every card wears its tier (a plain edge, an underline, a wax seal, an amber frame, a crimson glow)
and a band with the tier and the kind ("◆ SEALED · EMOTE"); what is yours carries a chip on its picture
(white "IN WHEEL · 1" or "WORN", outlined "OWNED"); a bought card is stamped YOURS with a ring of its
colour running out, and a card just worn or put on the wheel flares. Titles hang over the head in their
tier's look (`Rarity.titleLook`: no mark, a dot, a diamond; Condemned letters lean amber), the same on the
shop card and the results screen; on its shop card each title also wears its own emblem (Card.TITLE_ART: an icon of
the UI's atlas, large and faint behind the words and small in the tier's colour over them). The shop's window is lit
from the top by a soft round glow. Trails left the game on 2026-10-21.

These were agreed as the next things to sell, in no fixed order. Each keeps the game's rules: nothing
shows a role before the case gives it away, coins only unless the owner sets up a product, odds always
shown, and nothing ever sold for Robux by chance.
