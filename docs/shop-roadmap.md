# Death's hand: what comes next

The shop as it stands (2026-10-13): a table of tarot cards in suits, Today's hand dealt face down,
hold to buy, matching sets with titles, and styles that play in the case itself (writing, verdict,
death), Deathsinger forms and gestures. Coins only: the Robux suit stays hidden until a product id is
set in `src/shared/Products.luau`.

These were agreed as the next things to sell, in no fixed order. Each keeps the game's rules: nothing
shows a role before the case gives it away, coins only unless the owner sets up a product, odds always
shown, and nothing ever sold for Robux by chance.

| Idea | What it is | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Role reveal intros | How your role card turns over at the start of a case (a flip, a burn, ink bleeding through) | Seen by you alone: the reveal is private |
| Win scenes | A short pose or scene on the results screen when your side wins | After the case, so it gives nothing away |
| Manual covers on the real book | The cover you own on Death's Manual in the cutscenes, not only on the notebook | Diavolo's alone, so shown only once Diavolo is out |
| Animated titles | Titles whose letters move (a flicker, a slow ink fill) in the lobby and on the results screen | Lobby and results only, like every title |
| Portrait frames | A frame round your portrait on the profile card and the leaderboards | |
| Lobby marionette companion | A small puppet that follows you round the lobby on its strings | Lobby only |
| Season ledger | A season's track of rewards for playing, with a free line and (later) a paid one | The paid line needs a Robux product: owner's call |
| Fate draw | A draw for a random item from a shown pool, paid in coins | Coins only, odds shown, no duplicates, never Robux |
