# Death's hand: what comes next

The shop as it stands (2026-10-14): a table of tarot cards in suits, Today's hand dealt face down with
the day's deal at a quarter off, hold to buy, matching sets with titles (emote sets too), collection
milestones, and styles that play in the case itself (writing, verdict, death), Deathsinger forms and
the emote pack. Browsing: search, show and order, "New" ribbons, posed emote cards, a wishlist. Win
scenes, Fate's weekly draw and the Season ledger's free line came with the emote pack, and a mannequin
by the lobby spawn shows the day's emote. Coins only: the Robux suit stays hidden until a product id
is set in `src/shared/Products.luau`.

These were agreed as the next things to sell, in no fixed order. Each keeps the game's rules: nothing
shows a role before the case gives it away, coins only unless the owner sets up a product, odds always
shown, and nothing ever sold for Robux by chance.
